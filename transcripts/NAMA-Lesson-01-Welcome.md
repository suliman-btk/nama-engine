# NAMA — Lesson 1: Welcome to NAMA Engine

**Audience:** Teachers
**Lesson outcome:** Describe how NAMA Engine turns a transcript into a lesson video.

## Pure narration

Welcome

NAMA Engine turns the transcript of a lesson into a complete video. You write what the narrator should say, and the engine does the rest.

The engine works in three steps. First, it records the voice. Next, it prepares slides that appear when each idea is spoken. Finally, it makes the video on your own computer.

Before the final video is made, you see pictures of many moments of the video. You check the logos, the colours and the text, and you confirm when everything looks right.

Thank you for watching. Your first lesson is only one transcript away.
